Advancing Multimodal Monitoring

Increasing maturation of Multi-Modal Monitoring (MMM) in neuro intensive care units is contributing more complete insight and therapeutic opportunities for improving management and outcome in severe traumatic brain injury and subarachnoid hemorrhage.

Hemedex strives to continually improve perfusion quantification technology, data analysis, and visualization to support MMM investigations to elevate standards of care for brain injured patients.
